## About

Something is wrong with the main menu.

**The Hollow** is a story-driven alternate reality game built entirely inside Geometry Dash. No external websites, no Discord puzzles, and no files to download. Only the game you already have, behaving in ways it was never meant to. Progress is saved on your device.

A quiet wrongness creeps in after install: a journal appears where nothing should be, clues accumulate in the dark, and familiar screens start to lie. You are not playing a new level pack. You are following a trail left by **the Hollow**, a presence that learns the layout of menus, icons, and buttons the same way you do.

The experience unfolds across the main menu and the places you already visit. Pay attention to what is missing, what is hidden, and what the game insists you should not touch yet.

## How to begin

 * Enable **The Hollow** and return to the main menu
 * Find the **journal** and open it. Read what it offers, then close it when you are ready
 * Press **Play** when the game allows you, then look everywhere else

There is no checklist in-game. If you are stuck, that is part of the design. Explore every corner of the UI you normally ignore.

## What to expect

 * A persistent story that advances when you do the right thing in the right place
 * Hidden collectibles scattered across menu screens
 * A journal that remembers what you have learned
 * Distortions when the Hollow pushes back
 * A final confrontation when you have seen enough

When the story ends, the mod steps aside and your game returns to normal.

## Settings

**Reset ARG progress** clears all Hollow flags, clues, and collectibles so you can start the story from the beginning. Use it for a clean run, a shared save, or if you want to experience the opening again.

## A fair warning

This mod is best played without spoilers. Hints from friends, videos, or wikis will blunt the point.

*Find the hollow before it finds you.*
